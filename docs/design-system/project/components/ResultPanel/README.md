# ResultPanel

What a viewer sees at the question on day 2, and what day-1 viewers see when they return: the winning choice as a `uw-verdict` (letter badge plus the choice in the story's words), the final split, and one primary button into the official branch. Use `uw-verdict--b` when B won. Say plainly who can still watch what: new viewers get only the official ending, while a viewer who watched the other ending on day 1 keeps it in their history. Late viewers may still open both glimpses from here. Label demo numbers as simulated.
